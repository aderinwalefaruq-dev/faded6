# Faded6 — Design System

## Vibe
- Lagos nightlife flyer × risograph screen-print: matte black stock, one electric ink, halftone grain, ticket-stub geometry.

## Color
- Primary: #C8FF2E
- On Primary: #0A0A0C
- Accent: #FF2E88
- On Accent: #0A0A0C
- Background: #0A0A0C
- Foreground: #F4F4F5
- Muted: #1A1A20
- Border: #2A2A33
- Secondary: #16161B

## Typography
- Heading: Space Grotesk (family: 'Space Grotesk', sans-serif, weight: 700, url: https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Space+Grotesk:wght@500;600;700&display=swap)
- Body: Inter (family: 'Inter', sans-serif, weight: 400, url: https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Inter:wght@400;500;600&display=swap)

## Visual Language
- Core visual signature: every brand surface carries a fine halftone dot grain and a 1px electric-lime hairline; ticket cards use punched notches and a dashed perforation so they read as physical stubs.
- Material & depth: matte black stock with no gloss — depth comes from surface lift (near-black to charcoal) plus a single lime glow under primary actions, never from soft drop shadows.
- Containers & buttons: charcoal panels with 1px slate borders and tight corners; primary actions are solid lime with near-black type and a lime halo on hover; secondary actions are charcoal fills with light type.
- Layout rhythm: lime appears only on actions, active states and micro-labels — never as a large field; dense uppercase micro-labels with wide tracking alternate with generous black breathing room.

## Animation
- Entrance: hero copy and cards fade up 8px over 400ms ease-out, staggered by 60ms.
- Interaction: primary buttons expand their lime halo over 150ms; cards lift 2px.
- Scroll / transition: sections fade in at 30% viewport intersection over 500ms.

## Forbidden
- No pastel or multi-hue gradients — the palette is black, one lime, one magenta.
- No soft rounded-pill buttons or large blurred shadows.
- No emoji anywhere in the interface.

## Additional Notes
- All user-visible copy is English; prices always render in Naira with the ₦ symbol.
- Login page uses a split-screen: full-bleed nightlife photograph on the left, form on matte black at the right.
- Ticket codes always render in monospace with the `F6-` prefix.
